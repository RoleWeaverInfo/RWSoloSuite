LOCAL AI SERVICE

Your adventures, brought to life.

Start Role Weaver before opening NWN. Keep the service window open, then launch NWN yourself and load your module or saved game.

Adventure packages register their own profile when you run their Start Role Weaver.cmd. Manual registration here preserves authoring drafts. Install the .mod and included HAKs before playing.

Talk to your characters

Choose “Talk freely with AI”, close the dialogue, then speak normally in Talk. You can also type !talk bram show me your goods, address Bram Ironstock: Hello, or use !ai your message. Use !end to finish.

AI conversations use your OpenAI or Gemini provider and key from Studio Settings. Keep NWN unpaused while waiting for replies. Native menus, authored conversations, quests and inventory remain available without live AI.
