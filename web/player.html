AI conversations, quests and merchants

Talk freely with characters, accept quests, choose authored outcomes and ask merchants to show their goods. NPC menu choices use the same game state. Purchases always require your click in the in-game goods panel.

Choose “Talk freely with AI”, close the dialogue, then speak normally in Talk. You can also type !talk bram show me your goods, address Bram Ironstock: Hello, or use !ai your message. Use !end to finish. Merchants require you to be within six metres and out of combat.

Uses the previously configured OPENAI_API_KEY environment variable. No credentials are contained in shared modules. Keep NWN unpaused while waiting for AI replies. Menus, quests and merchant panels work with the companion stopped.
